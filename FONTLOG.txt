FONTLOG for Civic Monoline

Civic Monoline (ໂຮງຮຽນ) is a display font for Lao, Thai, Khmer and Latin drawn from a hand-painted
sign photographed in Laos in 2019. Upright public-information lettering: even strokes, open forms, clear raised marks. School sign in Pakxong, Champasak.

How it was made
- Lao key letters (ໂ ນ ອ ະ ຸ ບ ປ ຖ ົ ມ ັ ດ ທ ຍ ຶ ສ າ ູ ຫ ຼ ກ 3 5 ິ ່ ື ຂ ຈ ໍ) were traced from the photograph (scripts/segment.py, scripts/trace.py).
- The other letters were generated on the Noto designs with a simulated brush whose size and the font's width were
  measured from the traced letters (scripts/auto.py), then merged with the traced letters (scripts/hybrid.py).
- Generated letters are drafts to be redrawn toward the traced ones before 1.0.
